Volume basis. Total MT is total quantity in kilograms divided by 1,000, taken only from value entries whose item ledger entry quantity is not zero. That condition is what counts each goods movement once: Business Central writes several value entries per shipment (original, cost adjustment, invoice reversal, invoice actual) and repeats the same kilogram figure on each, so summing every row overstates volume roughly twelvefold.
Amount basis. Revenue sums Sales_Amount_Actual_New + Sales_Amount_Expected_New and cost of sales sums Cost_Amount_Actual + Cost_Amount_Expected across all rows, so the expected amount posted on a shipment and its reversal on the invoice net to the true figure. Cost and volume are shown positive.
Scope. Sales shipments, invoices, return receipts and credit memos. Excludes source no. ZZZZZ and item codes beginning YY (delivery charges).
